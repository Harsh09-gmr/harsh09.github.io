Steps to prepare Tea

1. Boil Water: Fill a kettle or pot with fresh, cold water and heat it until it reaches a boil.

2.Prepare your cup or teapot: Place a tea bag or about one teaspoon of loose-leaf tea per cup into your mug or infuser.

3.Pour the water: Once the water has reached the desired temperature, pour it carefully over the tea leaves or the tea bag.

4.Steep: Let the tea steep for the recommended time, which typically ranges from 2 to 5 minutes depending on the type of tea.

5.Remove the tea: Remove the tea bag or strain out the loose leaves to prevent the tea from becoming too bitter.

6.Customize: Add milk, sugar, honey, or lemon to taste, if you prefer.
